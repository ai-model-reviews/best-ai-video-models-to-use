## Multiverse: put yourself into any motion

Multiverse is the feature I open most. It does three things:

- **Swap yourself into any motion.** Take a clip with the movement you want, like a dance, a walk-and-talk or a reaction. Put yourself or your character into it, and the motion stays.
- **Change the background.** Keep the performance and move it somewhere else: a rooftop, a studio, a neon street, a beach at golden hour.
- **Build new scenes from trending and viral content.** When something takes off, you make your own version of it with your face or your character in it, instead of filming it from scratch.

**How I run it:**

1. Pick a trending clip or upload my own motion reference.
2. Choose who goes in: me from a few photos, or a saved character.
3. Keep the original background or describe a new one.
4. Check the model and the token cost, then generate.

**Example: the trend, my way**

- **Motion reference:** a 10 second trending dance clip
- **Who goes in:** my saved character `@mila`
- **Background:** "a rooftop in Seoul at night, city lights, light rain"
- **Result:** the same choreography, my character, a scene nobody else posted

## Why I use Multiverse over Genjutsu

Higgsfield's Genjutsu does the same job. I moved to Multiverse for four reasons:

- **It's more permissive.** Fewer of my swaps get blocked, so I spend my time making the video instead of rewording the prompt.
- **It tells me which model I'm using.** I know exactly what's doing the work on every swap, so when a result looks great I can get it again.
- **It costs less.** The same kind of swap costs me less on Multiverse, and the token cost is on the button before I render.
- **It sits next to everything else.** The swap, the character, the edit and the extend all live on one balance. I don't have to export a clip and finish it somewhere else.

## Three workflows, one per creator type

### Social media creator: ride a trend in one sitting

1. **Multiverse:** put yourself into the trending motion from a few photos.
2. **Background:** move it somewhere that fits your niche.
3. **Seedance 2.5 Edit:** need a second version? Rewrite the setting with one sentence and keep the performance.
4. **Nano Banana Pro:** make the thumbnail with your face from reference photos.

```
Keep my performance and camera move exactly. Change the setting from my
living room to a packed outdoor night market, warm string lights, light haze.
```

### AI-native creator: one character, every trend

1. **Character Factory:** build your character once. Pick traits, upload up to five source photos, or remix one of 21 full-body blueprints. Then give it a voice from your voice library.
2. **Multiverse:** put that character into this week's trending motion.
3. **Kling 4.0:** have the character talk to camera with the same face and voice.

```
@mila looks into the camera mid-walk on a sunny boardwalk and says: "Okay,
I tried the trend. Rate it out of ten." Handheld, natural light, 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

Every plan includes 10 free character generations. [Build your first character free](https://socialsight.ai/?utm_source=github).

### Faceless channel operator: a week of posts without a real face

1. **Character Factory:** create a host character for the channel. It becomes the channel's face.
2. **Content creation agent:** give it a brief. It splits the brief into short shots, keeps the character and style across them, and shows the storyboard and token cost before anything renders.
3. **Multiverse:** drop the host into trending formats between your regular posts.

```
Five 30 second videos for a personal finance channel, one per weekday. Each
opens on a common money mistake, explains it in two sentences, and ends on one
fix. Calm, clear, 9:16. Host: @remy.
```
